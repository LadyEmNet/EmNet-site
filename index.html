EmNet Community Management Ltd has closed

After careful consideration, we’ve made the difficult decision to close EmNet Community Management Ltd.

Over the past few years, we’ve had the privilege of supporting projects and communities across the crypto space. We’re proud of the work delivered and the relationships built along the way. However, due to sustained changes in the broader market environment and the resulting impact on operations, it is no longer viable for us to continue.

We want to sincerely thank all clients, partners, and community members who have worked with us and supported us over the years.

— EmNet Community Management Ltd
