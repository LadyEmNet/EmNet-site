Our Services

EmNet provides professional, hands-on online content moderation for digital platforms operating in high-risk environments.

Core Moderation Services

Live Moderation

Active moderation across online platforms and community spaces. Enforcement of rules, removal of spam, and consistent presence to maintain stability.

Scam & Impersonation Control

Detection and removal of phishing attempts, impersonation accounts, malicious links, coordinated abuse, and harmful activity.

Conflict & Escalation Management

Handling disputes, managing volatile discussions, and maintaining structured communication during sensitive or high-pressure situations.

Reporting & Visibility

Provision of moderation summaries, enforcement transparency, and structured communication with platform teams where required.
